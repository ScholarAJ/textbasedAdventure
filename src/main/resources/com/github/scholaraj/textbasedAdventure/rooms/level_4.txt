1st_Floor
You step through the large, bulky double-doors of the castle foyer. You find yourself 
in a vast, open space surrounded by painted walls, each decorated with colorful symbols like
moons and stars. Above you is a dark blue ceiling speckled with glittering dots. The floor is 
tiled, with a glossy black-and-white checkerboard design. To the west is a tall staircase with 
red carpet and ornate rails. At the top of this staircase is another door, adorned with a large 
golden star and a strange octagonal keyhole. To the south is a spiral stone staircase leading 
to a dark cellar. To the north is a set of three more doors, but only a spruce door in the center 
appears useable. The others are boarded up and nailed down. To the east is the door you came through.
