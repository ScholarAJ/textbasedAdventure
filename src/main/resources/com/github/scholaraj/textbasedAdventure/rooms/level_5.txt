When you pass through the plain spruce door, you find yourself in an L-shaped corridor with
red carpet and pearly white walls. The walls are decorated with gold trim, as well as frames
of different shapes and sizes. One catches your eye. It's a small rectangular frame, but the
canvas appears totally blank. There's a bulge in the center, as if something is hidden 
behind it. Attached to the wall ext to it is a large torch. There is also a bookshelf 
around the corner, packed with books on various subjects such as alchemy, magic, and the 
history of different kingdoms. A golden book is slightly loose from the rest, as if someone 
had recently read it. Atop the bookshelf are a collection of vials, one of which is filled 
with a strange green liquid.
